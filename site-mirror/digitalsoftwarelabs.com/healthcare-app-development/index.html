We Create Mobile App Solutions for Healthcare that Adhere to Regulations.

Our mobile healthcare solutions are designed to comply with international and regional regulations, ensuring data privacy, legal accountability, and patient safety across every application we build for providers, clinics, and health institutions.

HIPAA

Digital Software Labs develops healthcare applications fully compliant with HIPAA, safeguarding electronic health data through encryption, secure access, and role-based permissions. This compliance is essential to our healthcare app development services, ensuring that sensitive patient information remains protected across mobile platforms, third-party integrations, and cloud-based storage systems.

HITECH (Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health)

All software built by Digital Software Labs meets HITECH standards, encouraging meaningful use of digital records and improving information exchange. Our experience in healthcare app development ensures our solutions qualify for federal incentives while maximizing efficiency, security, and clinical documentation accuracy within digital healthcare ecosystems.

SaMD (Software as a Medical Device)

As a leading healthcare app development company, Digital Software Labs designs SaMD-compliant platforms that support clinical decisions and diagnosis. These applications meet FDA and ISO standards, ensuring performance, traceability, and accuracy when used as stand-alone digital tools or integrated with broader care systems and medical hardware.

HL7 (Health Level Seven International)

Our apps follow HL7 protocols to ensure seamless data exchange between hospitals, labs, and government platforms. This framework, integral to modern app development healthcare, helps maintain interoperability, eliminate information silos, and standardize patient data across different healthcare providers, improving treatment coordination and reporting efficiency.

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

For clients operating in Europe, Digital Software Labs builds GDPR-compliant applications, securing patient data through user consent protocols and lawful data practices. Our Web Application Development commitment to international data regulations supports safe and compliant healthcare app development across borders, particularly in global clinical research and mobile health solutions.

SOC 2 (Service Organization Control 2)

SOC 2 compliance ensures our healthcare apps maintain data integrity, privacy, and operational reliability. At Digital Software Labs, we integrate SOC 2 practices into our healthcare app development services, delivering platforms that meet stringent security controls required for storing, transmitting, and processing protected health information.

CPT (Current Procedural Terminology)

Our healthcare applications support CPT coding for accurate medical billing and insurance claims. Digital Software Labs ensures full integration of CPT within its healthcare app development framework, improving reimbursement accuracy, workflow automation, and administrative efficiency in private practices and hospital billing departments.

GxP Quality Guidelines and Regulations

As a custom healthcare app development company, we adhere to GxP guidelines in every stage of design and deployment. These practices guarantee our software meets pharmaceutical and clinical safety standards, supporting traceable, validated systems essential for drug development, diagnostics, and clinical data management.

Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA)

We build applications that fully comply with PHIPA, ensuring lawful use and storage of personal health information in Canadian healthcare systems. Digital Software Labs incorporates this compliance into its healthcare app development process, supporting providers with region-specific safeguards and data governance protocols.
