Step 5: Enable Monetization Tools

Step 7: Preview and Publish

Once your page is set up, preview it to ensure everything is in place. Review all the links, appearance, and monetization options. After confirming everything is correct, publish your page and go live.

Step 8: Share Your Beacons Link

Share your Beacons AI link across your social media profiles, email newsletters, and other platforms. This will help drive traffic to your page and increase visibility, allowing you to grow your audience.
