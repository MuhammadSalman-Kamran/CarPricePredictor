Fixed Price:

In this format, the project scope, timeline, and cost are agreed upon upfront. The client pays a fixed amount for the entire project, regardless of the actual time and resources expended.

Time and Materials (T&M):

Under this format, the client is billed based on the actual time and materials used for the project. Rates are typically hourly or daily, with costs varying depending on the complexity and duration of the project.

FAQ

What is Mobile App Development?

Mobile app development is the process of designing, creating, and distributing applications for iOS, Android, and cross-platform devices. It entails UI/UX design, coding, testing, and integration to deliver easy, scalable, and high-performance mobile solutions customized to business requirements.

How Long Does It Take to Develop an App?

The development time for an app varies according to its complexity, features, and functionality needs. Design, testing, and platform compatibility all influence the process. A well-structured development methodology guarantees that tasks are completed efficiently and on time while maintaining quality and performance.
